Built around trust, clarity and safer meetings

Book A Partner is intended for respectful, platonic companionship and everyday assistance. Clear profiles, defined services and a customer approval step help keep the experience transparent.

🛡️ Profile verification review Partner profiles can include profile photo, selfie, age, mobile and service details for review.
📍 Public meeting locations Customers should prefer safe, public meeting locations for bookings.
🤝 Respectful, platonic service Harassment, threats, abuse or adult/escort services are not permitted.
