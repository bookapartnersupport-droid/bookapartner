Refund & Cancellation

Provider timing: Refund initiation target is within 1 hour where operationally possible; the final credit time depends on the payment provider.

1. Before partner acceptance

Customer cancellation before partner acceptance: 100% refund. Partner rejection: 100% refund. If the partner does not respond within the applicable 2-hour response window, the request may auto-expire and receive a full refund.

2. Customer cancellation after acceptance

Time before scheduled startRefund
24 hours or more100%
12 to less than 24 hours75%
6 to less than 12 hours50%
Less than 6 hours0%

3. Partner cancellation / no-show

Partner cancellation or partner no-show: 100% refund to the customer, subject to payment-provider processing. The platform may record the violation and apply operational review or disciplinary action for repeated incidents.

4. Customer no-show

Customer no-show normally receives no refund; the partner may become payout-eligible subject to evidence and applicable admin review.

5. Late arrival / grace period

A 15-minute grace period applies. A significant delay may be reported and can result in cancellation/refund or an actual-time adjustment after review.

6. Early completion

Early completion does not automatically create a refund. If the partner ends materially early, the customer may report an issue for review.

7. Disputes

When a dispute is active, the relevant partner payout may be held. Evidence may be requested. Admin review determines the applicable settlement. Appeals should be submitted within 48 hours where the applicable process permits.

8. Full-refund treatment

Where a full refund is eligible, the refund is intended to include the amount charged under the platform's booking flow, including applicable platform commission, and the partner/platform should retain zero from that eligible transaction. Eligible full refunds should not deduct a gateway fee merely because the payment was processed, subject to provider capability and applicable law.

9. Payment-provider dependency

Book A Partner does not promise a bank-credit timestamp it cannot control. Refund status should be shown as initiated/processing/completed/failed or equivalent when supported.

10. Policy version

MVP draft. Effective date and final legal wording must be inserted after legal review.
