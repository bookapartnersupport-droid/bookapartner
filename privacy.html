Privacy Policy

Production note: This is an MVP policy draft and should be reviewed by qualified legal counsel before production launch.

1. Information collected

Depending on the service used, Book A Partner may collect account details such as name, email, mobile number, date of birth/age eligibility, city/area, profile photo, partner service details, verification information, booking details, payment references, messages, reviews, complaints, safety reports and technical/security information needed to operate the platform.

2. Purpose

Information may be used for account creation, eligibility, partner verification, search and matching, booking, communication, payment/refund reconciliation, safety, fraud prevention, customer support, dispute handling, service improvement and legal compliance.

3. Sensitive and private information

KYC, identity proofs, police/background verification information, private addresses, payment credentials and security credentials should be access-controlled and shown only to authorized roles or service providers that require the information. Card numbers, CVV and UPI PIN should not be stored by the application.

4. Location

Location information should be collected only when needed for search, service delivery, safety or routing and according to the applicable consent and privacy controls. Private booking addresses are not public profile information.

5. Communications

Platform messages and booking communications may be processed to deliver the service, prevent abuse, support disputes and maintain required operational records, subject to applicable law and access controls.

6. Sharing

Information may be shared with the other booking party only to the extent necessary for the confirmed service, with payment/hosting/communication/verification providers needed to operate the service, or where legally required. Book A Partner should not sell personal data.

7. Retention and deletion

Users may request deletion of their account. Deletion may be delayed or limited where active bookings, disputes, payouts, fraud/safety investigations, legal obligations or required record retention apply.

8. Security

The platform should use role-based access, secure authentication, row-level access controls, private storage for sensitive documents, minimal data exposure and appropriate logging. Users must protect their own credentials.

9. Third-party services

Payment gateways, cloud hosting, authentication, mapping, communication and verification providers may process relevant information under their own policies and contractual safeguards.

10. Children's data

Book A Partner is intended for users aged 18 or older. The platform should not knowingly onboard minors.

11. Rights and grievances

Users may contact support regarding access, correction, deletion, privacy concerns or complaints. The final production policy should provide the official privacy/grievance contact details and applicable response process.

12. Changes

Material changes should be published with a new policy version and effective date and, where required, renewed consent.
