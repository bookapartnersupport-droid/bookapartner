Partner Agreement

Production note: This is an MVP agreement draft and should be reviewed by qualified Indian legal counsel before partners are onboarded commercially.

1. Partner eligibility

Partner must be 18+, provide accurate identity/contact information, complete required KYC and any applicable background/police checks, complete the platform's call/review process and receive approval before becoming Live.

2. Services

Partner may initially list up to five approved services. Specialized services require the qualification/proof specified by the platform. Partner must provide services only within the approved scope.

3. Pricing

The platform may apply the published duration discounts: 2h 5%, 3h 10%, 4h 15%, and 5h+ 20% maximum mandatory duration discount. Transport rules may include 10 km free and a maximum transport charge of ₹150 where applicable. The partner sees expected earnings before accepting a request.

4. Commission

The current platform commission is 15% of retained/eligible transaction revenue. Example: ₹1,000 eligible revenue produces ₹150 commission and ₹850 partner net before any separately disclosed applicable adjustments. Future commission changes should be communicated before taking effect.

5. Booking acceptance

Partner should respond to a booking request within two hours. Partner acceptance creates the confirmed booking according to the platform flow. The partner must honor confirmed bookings except for legitimate emergencies or approved changes.

6. Cancellation and no-show

Partner cancellation normally results in a full customer refund. Partner no-show is a serious operational violation and may lead to refund, evidence review, warning, suspension or other action. Repeated violations may lead to deactivation or ban.

7. Arrival and service conduct

A 15-minute arrival grace period applies. Significant delay should be communicated through the platform and may be reported. Partner must behave professionally, respect boundaries, avoid harassment, threats, illegal activity, unsafe transport/driving and intoxicated service delivery.

8. Payment and off-platform transactions

Payments for platform bookings must remain within the supported platform/payment flow. Asking a customer to bypass the platform payment or manipulating booking/payment records is prohibited.

9. Completion and payout

After scheduled completion and the applicable confirmation window, the booking may become payout-eligible unless there is an active issue/dispute. Normal payout target is 6–8 hours after completion, subject to processing, verification and absence of a hold. No payout is guaranteed while an active dispute or payment/reconciliation issue exists.

10. Safety

Partner may use SOS/112 or appropriate emergency services for immediate danger and the platform's Report Customer/Block Customer safety tools. Book A Partner is not an emergency response provider.

11. Ratings and reviews

Partners may rate completed bookings subject to the platform's review rules. Manipulation, retaliation, threats, fake reviews and privacy violations are prohibited.

12. Verification and badge

A verification badge represents recorded platform checks and does not guarantee the partner's future behavior, safety or service quality.

13. Suspension and appeals

Partner status may move from Live to Warning, Suspended, Deactivated or Banned based on documented operational, safety, payment or policy concerns. The platform should provide a reason and applicable review/appeal path.

14. Exit

Partner account closure may occur after pending bookings, disputes and payouts are resolved, subject to required record retention and fresh verification requirements for any future re-entry.

15. Taxes and independent status

The final production agreement should define applicable tax responsibilities and the legal relationship between platform and partner after professional legal/accounting review. Nothing in this MVP draft should be treated as establishing a particular employment or agency relationship without that review.

16. Effective date

To be inserted after legal review and production approval.
